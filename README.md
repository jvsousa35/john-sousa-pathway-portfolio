## Project Overview

This is my final project portfolio website. The goal of the project was to create an aspirational professional portfolio that represents the person I want to become professionally.

My chosen professional direction is **Head of College Pathway & Player Recruitment**. This portfolio is built around my interest in helping academy soccer players turn their potential into real college and professional opportunities.

The site presents a future-facing but realistic version of my professional identity. It connects my current work in soccer with the larger role I want to grow into at the MLS, European academy, or academy director level.

---

## Professional Direction

My professional goal is to build organized player pathway systems for academy-level players. I want to work in the space between player development, college recruitment, scouting, family education, player profiling, and long-term opportunity building.

The main problem I want to help solve is the gap between **player potential** and **player opportunity**. Many talented players, especially players from underprivileged or financially limited backgrounds, have the ability to reach the next level but may not always have access to the right information, network, exposure, or guidance.

This portfolio shows the kind of work I want to be known for:

- Helping players with college recruitment
- Building player profiles and recruitment materials
- Connecting players with college coaches
- Identifying realistic college and professional opportunities
- Creating pathway education meetings for players and families
- Supporting players through communication, exposure, and honest guidance
- Building stronger relationships between academy clubs and college programs
- Creating long-term player pathways that extend beyond youth soccer

## Research and Planning

Before building the site, I planned the professional direction I wanted to present, the audience for the portfolio, the type of projects that would make sense, and the tone/design style that would fit the role.

The portfolio is based on a realistic player pathway and recruitment model. The pathway work focuses on:

- education
- exposure
- communication
- realistic guidance
- college coach relationships
- player ownership
- long-term opportunity building

The site was designed to feel like a serious soccer academy portfolio, not a random personal website. The visual style uses a European club-inspired direction with a dark background, gold accents, structured layouts, and real soccer imagery.
