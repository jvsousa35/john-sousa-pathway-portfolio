Case Study 01

College Pathway Department Model

Problem: Many players and families do not have a clear understanding of the college recruitment process, which creates confusion, missed timelines, and unrealistic expectations.

Process: Build a department model centered on education, exposure, communication, realistic guidance, and college coach relationships.

Outcome: A clearer structure for how the club supports players from U15 through U19 while still making players take ownership of their recruitment.

Case Study 02

Player Recruitment Profile System

Problem: Talented players are not always presented in a way that is easy for college coaches to evaluate quickly.

Process: Create a repeatable player profile structure that includes player bio, academics, positions, video links, coach notes, strengths, development areas, and target school lists.

Outcome: A more organized and coach-friendly way to communicate a player's background, level, and recruitment status.

Case Study 03

Showcase & College Coach Outreach Workflow

Problem: Players often attend showcases without a clear outreach plan, target school list, or follow-up structure.

Process: Identify player goals, match players with realistic schools, contact coaches before events, share schedules and player profiles, track responses, and follow up after the event.

Outcome: A more intentional event process where exposure is planned instead of left to chance.

Case Study 04

Player & Parent Education Program

Problem: Players and families often have different expectations about recruitment, scholarships, communication, and what the club can realistically provide.

Process: Build education nights, workshops, Q&A sessions, communication guidance, financial reality conversations, and school-fit education.

Outcome: Families become more informed, players take more ownership, and the club creates a clearer support structure.
