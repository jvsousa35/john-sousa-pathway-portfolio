Case Study 01

College Pathway Department Model

Goal: Create a clear club-wide recruitment process that supports players and families through education, exposure, communication, realistic guidance, and college coach relationships.

Why it matters: Players should not have to guess their way through recruitment. A department model gives the club a repeatable structure and gives families a clearer understanding of the process.

Case Study 03

Showcase & College Coach Outreach Workflow

Goal: Turn showcases into intentional recruitment moments instead of random exposure. The workflow starts with player interests, matches players with schools, contacts coaches, shares schedules, and tracks follow-ups.

Impact: The club can make outreach more targeted, players can understand who is watching, and families can see the difference between just attending an event and preparing for one.
