AI Workflow

How AI was used intentionally.

AI was used as a planning, writing, organization, and coding assistant. The goal was not to replace decision-making, but to help turn real pathway ideas into a more professional portfolio structure.

1

Assignment Breakdown

I used AI to interpret the final project requirements and identify what the portfolio needed to show: professional direction, research, planning, projects, skills, and AI workflow.

2

Professional Direction

I used AI to refine the role from a general soccer data idea into a more personal and believable direction: Head of College Pathway & Player Recruitment.

3

Content Organization

I gave AI real notes from my pathway plan, parent education ideas, and role responsibilities. AI helped organize those ideas into pages, sections, and case studies.

4

Design Direction

I chose a European club-style visual direction. AI helped translate that into a dark, polished, academy-inspired layout with gold accents, cards, timelines, and professional imagery.

5

Website Build

AI generated the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, README, and folder structure. I reviewed the output so the site matched my actual work, voice, and professional goals.

Research First

The site is based on a realistic role direction that connects recruitment, player development, college pathways, analysis, and academy leadership.

Planning Second

The portfolio was planned as a multi-page system, not just a landing page, so it could explain the problem, pathway, projects, and process clearly.

Build Third

The final site uses a simple GitHub Pages structure so it can be hosted easily while still feeling polished and presentation-ready.
