AI Workflow

How AI supported the project.

AI was used as a planning and production assistant, not as a replacement for decision-making.

1

Assignment Understanding

I used AI to break down the final project requirements and identify what the site needed to show: professional direction, planning, projects, design, and AI process.

2

Professional Direction

I used AI to organize my role into a clear portfolio identity: Head of College Pathway & Player Recruitment, with a future goal of working at larger academy platforms.

3

Content Refinement

I gave rough notes about my role, responsibilities, challenges, and pathway ideas, then used AI to help turn them into structured website sections and case studies.

4

Design and Development

I used AI to help generate the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, page structure, visual direction, and README content for a GitHub Pages portfolio site.

5

Human Review

I reviewed, adjusted, and directed the output so the final site matched my real goals, experience, tone, and professional direction.

Research & Planning

Design decisions behind the site.

The portfolio was planned around a realistic professional direction, not just a visual design.

Professional Research

I studied the type of work connected to academy recruitment, college pathway development, player profiling, coach communication, scouting, and long-term player support.

Audience Planning

The site was designed for academy directors, MLS academy staff, college coaches, soccer operations leaders, and recruitment departments who would want to understand my direction quickly.

Visual Direction

I chose a European club-inspired style because it fits the serious academy environment I want the portfolio to represent: dark background, gold accents, structured layouts, and real soccer imagery.

Content Strategy

The portfolio focuses on systems instead of just personal achievements. The goal was to show how player data, recruitment tools, education, exposure, and coach relationships can work together.
