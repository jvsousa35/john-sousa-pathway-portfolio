Professional Direction

Head of College Pathway & Player Recruitment.

My work focuses on helping players and families understand the recruitment process, build strong player profiles, connect with college coaches, and identify realistic opportunities that fit each player’s level, goals, academics, finances, and future.

The Problem

Talent alone does not always create access.

Many talented players, especially underprivileged players, have the ability to reach the next level but may lack exposure, resources, guidance, or the right network. My goal is to bridge the gap between player potential and opportunity.

Pathway Preview

A system built around clarity and opportunity.

The portfolio is organized around the type of department model I want to keep building: player data, recruitment tools, coach relationships, education, exposure, and long-term connection.

Player Profiles

Organizing player bio, academic information, video links, coach notes, position details, and target school lists.

Coach Relationships

Developing trust with college programs and helping coaches understand the players within the academy environment.

Showcase Execution

Preparing outreach, schedules, player lists, and follow-up systems around major events and recruitment windows.

Player Education

Teaching players how to communicate, build highlight videos, research schools, and take ownership of the process.

Parent Education

Clarifying expectations, financial realities, right fit, and the roles of player, family, and club.

Post-Grad Connection

Keeping players connected through commitments, alumni culture, UPSL, USL2, training, mentorship, and coaching opportunities.
